# Shabbos Mode design direction

- Keep on-screen text **very minimal**. Use only words needed to identify a screen, explain an action, or give essential feedback. Avoid filler, numbered sections, repeated descriptions, and multiple taglines.
- Keep the interface minimal: generous empty space, a small number of clear elements, and one obvious primary action per screen.
- Use a warm brown and paper palette with strong contrast. Pixel details should be restrained accents, not a layer over every surface.
- Use the bundled Plex serif for headings and prominent times, sans for actions and body copy, and mono for compact schedule details. Favor ruled lists, flat fields, and restrained cut corners. The sleep screen defaults to true black for OLED.
- The two pixel candles are the app logo. Keep their square geometry consistent in the launcher icon and app header.
- Add thoughtful, simple animations or transitions when they clarify state or add a quiet sense of life. Pixel motion is welcome. Keep motion subtle, brief, and nonessential to understanding or using the app.
- Preserve accessibility: readable text, clear tap targets, and support for reduced motion where practical.
