## Saved times and simpler preparation

- Minyan times can be minutes before or after candle lighting. They follow the configured candle time and weekly overrides across Home, the clock, and Tasker scheduling.
- Todo list has its own screen. Minyan confirmation lives in Shuls.
- Alarm Save ignores repeated taps and retains the saved record for scheduling retries. The original duplicate report remains unconfirmed; existing alarms are retained.
- City search accepts inputs such as “Spring Valley NY” and distinguishes places with matching names.
- Added the 15-minute candle-lighting preset and community labels for daytime and Shabbos-ending choices.
- Todo reminders open the right screen even when the app is already running.

### Installing

Download `shabbos-mode-0.1.3.apk` from the assets below. The `.sha256` asset contains its checksum.

This uses the same release key as 0.1.2 and updates that signed release in place. Existing local data is preserved. Relative minyan times need the required week's cached candle-lighting time; unavailable times are not guessed.

Earlier debug installs use a different signing key and cannot update in place with this APK. Uninstalling deletes their local data.
